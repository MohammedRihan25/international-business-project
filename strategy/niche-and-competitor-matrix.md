---

## 1. Niche Problem-Solution Thesis

### The Core Customer Problem
1. **Messy & Wasteful Application:** Applying rosemary oil, minoxidil, or hair-growth peptides using traditional droppers causes greasy hands, dripping along the forehead/neck, and 60–70% of serum absorption trapped in the hair shafts rather than reaching the dermal papilla (scalp follicles).
2. **Expensive Clinic Treatments:** Red light phototherapy sessions at Western aesthetic clinics cost $75–$150 per session.
3. **Inconsistent Scalp Stimulation:** Manual scalp massage is tiring and uneven.

### The Product Solution
* **Integrated 6ml Serum Chamber & Rolling Ball Teeth:** Dispenses micro-droplets directly to the scalp roots upon gentle pressure—zero grease on hands or hair length.
* **650nm Medical-Grade Red Light Diode Array:** Stimulates cellular ATP in hair follicles to prolong the anagen (growth) phase.
* **High-Frequency Micro-Vibration (10,000 RPM):** Relaxes scalp tension and increases micro-circulation to maximize nutrient uptake.
* **IPX5 Waterproof & USB-C Rechargeable:** Easy to rinse clean under tap water.
