## 2. Working Capital Float & Cash-Flow Runway

When starting with near-zero capital, the primary constraint is the **payout hold delay** (typically 7–10 days until delivery confirmation clears PayPal's rolling reserve).

### Working Capital Float Requirements Table

| Daily Order Pace | Orders in 7-Day Cycle | Total Landed COGS Required | Founder Out-of-Pocket Float Needed | Strategy |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **1 Order / Day** | 7 orders | $94.50 (~₹8,030) | **~$80 – $95** | Covered via personal savings or standard Indian credit card. |
| **3 Orders / Day** | 21 orders | $283.50 (~₹24,090) | **~$280** | Fulfill with 45-day credit card; first payouts arrive before statement due date. |
| **5 Orders / Day** | 35 orders | $472.50 (~₹40,160) | **~$470** | Payout releases start recycling daily; self-sustaining capital loop. |
| **10 Orders / Day**| 70 orders | $945.00 (~₹80,325) | **~$945** | Store is generating >$218/day (~₹18,500/day) in clear net profit. |

> **The Zero-Capital Credit Card Float Hack:**
> Using a standard Indian personal credit card (HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis) with a 45–50 day interest-free period:
> 1. You charge CJdropshipping supplier orders to the credit card in USD ($13.50/order).
> 2. Customers pay via PayPal/Razorpay.
> 3. Within 7–10 days, tracking is marked "Delivered" and PayPal releases the full $39.99 into your bank account.
> 4. You pay off the credit card statement before any interest is charged.
> 5. **Net Out-of-Pocket Cash Spent = $0.00.**
