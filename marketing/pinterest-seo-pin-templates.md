## 4. Enabling Pinterest Rich Pins (Free Schema Integration)

Rich Pins pull live pricing, product availability ("In Stock"), and store title automatically from your website.
* We have added Open Graph and Product Schema markup to the `/storefront/index.html` file (`og:price:amount`, `og:price:currency`, `product:availability`).
* Validate your storefront once live using Pinterest's official free tool: `developers.pinterest.com/tools/url-debugger/`.
