### Step 1: Form the Wyoming LLC
* Use an established registered agent service (such as *Northwest Registered Agent*, *Wyoming Registered Agent*, or *Firstbase/Doola*).
* Alternatively, file directly on the Wyoming Secretary of State website (`wyobiz.wyo.gov`) using a registered agent's commercial address for **$102**.
* Structure: **Single-Member Foreign-Owned Disregarded Entity LLC**.

### Step 2: Obtain an IRS EIN (Employer Identification Number)
* As a non-US resident without an SSN or ITIN, you cannot apply online through the IRS web portal.
* **The Free Protocol:** Complete **IRS Form SS-4**.
  * Line 7b: Write "Foreign" (leave SSN blank).
  * Line 9a: Check "Other" -> Write "Sole Member Disregarded Entity".
  * Line 10: "Started new business - E-Commerce cross-border retail".
* **Transmission:** Fax Form SS-4 to the IRS international fax number: `+1-855-641-6935`.
* The IRS issues your official EIN confirmation letter (Form CP-575 or 147C) within 4–7 business days via return fax or mail.

### Step 3: Open a US Business Bank Account (Mercury / Relay Financial)
* Both Mercury Bank and Relay Financial cater specifically to foreign-owned US LLCs.
* **Requirements:**
  1. Approved Wyoming Articles of Organization.
  2. IRS EIN confirmation document (Form CP575 / 147C).
  3. Indian Passport of the founder (as valid photo ID).
  4. Operating Agreement of the LLC.
  5. Live store website URL showing USD pricing and active terms.
* **Cost:** $0 setup fee, $0 monthly maintenance fee, FDIC-insured up to $5M through partner banks (Choice Financial / Evolve Bank).

### Step 4: Connect US Stripe
* Register at `stripe.com` -> Select country: **United States**.
* Enter your Wyoming LLC legal name and EIN.
* Enter your registered agent's US commercial address.
* Connect the Mercury/Relay Bank Routing Number and Checking Account Number.
* Verify your identity using your Indian Passport.
* **Immediate Benefits:**
  * One-click checkout with Apple Pay & Google Pay.
  * Direct 2-day rolling payout to your US bank account.
  * Pay CJdropshipping suppliers directly in USD from your US checking card (zero FX conversion fee!).

## 3. Mandatory Annual IRS Compliance (Form 5472 & Form 1120)

> **CRITICAL WARNING:** Even though a foreign-owned single-member LLC with no US employees or real estate has **0% US Federal Income Tax liability**, the IRS mandates an annual informational return.

* **Forms:** **Form 1120** (Pro Forma) and **Form 5472** (Information Return of a 25% Foreign-Owned U.S. Corporation or Foreign-Owned U.S. DE).
* **Deadline:** April 15 of every calendar year.
* **Penalty for Failure to File:** **$25,000 fine per violation**.
* **Filing Cost:** Free if self-filed using IRS instructions, or ~$200–$350 using a specialized CPA or service like StartGlobal/Doola. Always budget this from your ongoing profits.

## 4. Anti-Suspension Rules for Non-Resident Stripe Accounts

1. **Never use cheap VPNs or Datacenter Proxies:** Access Stripe dashboard using your direct Indian ISP IP or a clean, dedicated residential IP. Stripe flags logins originating from known VPN servers.
2. **Dispute Rate Threshold:** Keep chargebacks strictly under **0.75%**. Set up automatic refund rules on dispute alerts.
3. **Descriptor Optimization:** Set customer credit card statement descriptor to: `BRANDNAME* COM` and add your customer support phone/email. When a customer recognizes the charge on their bank app, they don't report it as fraud.
