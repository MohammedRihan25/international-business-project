## 2. GST & LUT (Form RFD-11) Zero-Rated Export SOP

Under Indian Goods and Services Tax (GST) law:
* **Export of Physical Goods/Services** is treated as **Zero-Rated Supply** under Section 16 of the IGST Act, 2017.
* **The Problem:** Without an active LUT, exporters must pay 18% Integrated GST (IGST) upfront on every transaction and file complex refund claims with the government.
* **The Solution:** File an annual **Letter of Undertaking (LUT) via Form GST RFD-11** on the official GST portal (`gst.gov.in`).

### Step-by-Step LUT Filing (100% Free, Completed in 10 Minutes):
1. Log in to `gst.gov.in` using your GST credentials.
2. Navigate to: `Services` -> `User Services` -> `Furnish Letter of Undertaking (LUT)`.
3. Select the Financial Year (e.g., 2026–2027).
4. Upload details of two witnesses (Name, Address, Occupation).
5. Accept declarations confirming you will export goods/services in compliance with FEMA rules.
6. Sign using Aadhaar OTP (Electronic Verification Code - EVC).
7. **Instant Result:** An official ARN (Application Reference Number) is generated. You can now invoice and accept international export payments at **0% GST** completely legally!

## 3. DGFT Import Export Code (IEC) Protocol

* **What it is:** A 10-digit code issued by the Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT), Ministry of Commerce.
* **When you need it:** Once your store crosses ₹20 Lakhs in annual turnover, or when your Indian bank requests formal export proof for clearing high-volume inward foreign remittances.
* **How to Apply ($0–₹500):**
  1. Go to `dgft.gov.in`.
  2. Click on **Apply for IEC**.
  3. Register with your PAN, Aadhaar, and active bank account details.
  4. Pay the government fee of ₹500 online.
  5. The IEC is issued digitally within 24 hours.

## 4. Foreign Inward Remittance Advice (e-FIRA)

* **Why it matters:** An e-FIRA is the single most critical document required by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) and Indian Income Tax Department. It certifies that funds entering your bank account are from international export buyers in foreign currency (USD), not domestic untaxed cash.
* **How PayPal e-FIRA Works:**
  * Inward settlements are routed through partner authorized dealer banks (e.g., Citi / HSBC).
  * The nodal bank files an EDPMS (Export Data Processing and Monitoring System) entry with RBI and generates a digital FIRA with a unique IRM (Inward Remittance Message) reference.
  * Download these certificates monthly from your PayPal Merchant dashboard under `Reports -> Statements -> FIRA`.

## 5. Income Tax Filing Strategy (Section 44AD / 44ADA)

* As a Sole Proprietor in India, your dropshipping net profit is taxed under your personal individual slab rates.
* **Presumptive Taxation Scheme (Section 44AD):**
  * If your turnover is under ₹2 Crores, you can opt for Section 44AD of the Income Tax Act.
  * Under 44AD, you are not required to maintain complex audited books of accounts.
  * You declare a presumptive net profit of **6% (for digital payments)** of your total turnover and pay income tax on that amount according to your individual income tax slab.
