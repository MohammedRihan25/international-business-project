# Chargeback Defense & Dispute Rebuttal Templates
## High-Win-Rate Templates for PayPal, Stripe, and Credit Card Issuers

Cross-border disputes typically fall into two categories:
1. **Item Not Received (INR)**
2. **Significantly Not as Described (SNAD)** / Unrecognized Transaction

Use these exact rebuttal letters to win disputes and protect your merchant standing.

---

## Template 1: Item Not Received (INR) Rebuttal

**Scenario:** Customer claims the package never arrived while it is in transit or after USPS marks it delivered.

```markdown
To: Merchant Review Department / Dispute Resolution Specialist
Case ID: [INSERT CASE / TRANSACTION ID]
Customer Name: [CUSTOMER FULL NAME]
Transaction Date: [DATE]
Delivery Carrier: USPS / CJPacket Sensitive
Tracking Number: [INSERT TRACKING NUMBER]
Carrier Tracking Link: https://tools.usps.com/go/TrackConfirmAction?tLabels=[TRACKING_NUMBER]

Dear Dispute Resolution Specialist,

We are submitting formal proof of fulfillment and delivery to refute the buyer's claim of "Item Not Received."

1. ORDER DETAILS & ADDRESS VERIFICATION:
The buyer placed an order for the 3-in-1 Scalp Massager & Applicator Comb on [ORDER DATE]. 
The order was shipped strictly to the verified billing/shipping address submitted by the customer at checkout:
[INSERT CUSTOMER FULL ADDRESS]

2. CARRIER PROOF & TRACKING TIMELINE:
The package was dispatched via USPS tracked service under tracking number [TRACKING NUMBER]. 
Carrier event logs indicate:
- [DATE]: Package accepted and scanned into transit.
- [DATE]: Arrival at Destination Unit / Local Post Office.
- [DATE]: DELIVERED at [TIMESTAMP], "Delivered, In/At Mailbox / Front Porch" at the destination address provided.

3. CUSTOMER COMMUNICATIONS:
Our store shipping policy explicitly states international delivery timeframes of 5–10 business days. Automated shipping notifications with active tracking links were delivered to the customer's email ([CUSTOMER EMAIL]) on [DATE].

As the tracking record independently confirms physical delivery to the customer's verified address by the national postal carrier (USPS), we respectfully request that this dispute be decided in our favor and all held funds released.

Sincerely,
Merchant Support Team
[STORE NAME] / [STORE URL]
```

## Template 2: Significantly Not as Described (SNAD) Rebuttal

**Scenario:** Customer claims the product is different from what was advertised or does not meet expectations.

```markdown
To: Dispute Adjudication Team
Case ID: [INSERT CASE ID]
Product: 3-in-1 Red Light Scalp Massager & Serum Applicator Comb
Store URL: [STORE URL]

Dear Adjudication Officer,

We are submitting comprehensive evidence demonstrating that the merchandise delivered to the customer strictly matches the product specifications, imagery, and operational claims advertised on our storefront.

1. EXACT SPECIFICATION COMPLIANCE:
The delivered product corresponds precisely to the advertised unit:
- Feature 1: Built-in 6ml liquid reservoir with rolling ball applicator teeth (Matches listing).
- Feature 2: 650nm Red LED light phototherapy diodes (Matches listing).
- Feature 3: USB-C rechargeable micro-vibration motor (Matches listing).

2. RETURN POLICY & RESOLUTION ATTEMPT:
Our published Return Policy states that customers may request an exchange or return for store credit or refund within 30 days by contacting our support team at [SUPPORT EMAIL].
- Prior to filing this claim, the buyer did not reach out to our customer care team to request troubleshooting or initiate a formal return.
- We have reiterated our offer to the customer to replace any unit they perceive as defective upon return or video demonstration.

3. REQUEST FOR ITEM RETURN:
If the buyer insists on a refund under buyer protection rules, we request that the buyer be required to return the merchandise with a valid tracking number to our designated returns facility prior to any refund disbursement, pursuant to standard merchant terms.

Thank you for your fair consideration.

Sincerely,
[STORE NAME] Management
```

## Template 3: Unrecognized Transaction / Fraud Claim Rebuttal

**Scenario:** Cardholder claims they did not authorize the charge.

```markdown
To: Fraud Investigation Department
Case Reference: [CASE ID]
Disputed Amount: $[AMOUNT] USD

Dear Fraud Review Team,

We are providing transactional and digital footprint verification confirming that this transaction was authorized by the cardholder:

1. DIGITAL FOOTPRINT MATCH:
- IP Address at Checkout: [CUSTOMER IP]
- Geolocation of IP: Matches Billing City & Zip Code ([CITY, STATE, ZIP])
- Device Fingerprint: [IOS / ANDROID / BROWSER DETAILS]
- Email Address: [CUSTOMER EMAIL] (Matches customer name)

2. SHIPPING ADDRESS MATCH:
The item was delivered directly to the cardholder's residential address on file:
[FULL ADDRESS]
Proof of USPS Delivery: [LINK TO CARRIER TRACKING]

Because the delivery address and IP location directly align with the cardholder's verified billing credentials, there is no evidence of third-party card skimming or identity compromise. We request that this chargeback be reversed.

Respectfully,
[STORE NAME]
```
