# Shipping & Delivery Policy
**Last Updated: November 2026**

At FolliRoot Labs, we are committed to providing reliable, transparent, and expedited shipping services to our customers across the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, and Australia.

---

## 1. Order Processing Time
* All orders are processed, quality-inspected, and dispatched within **24 to 48 hours** (1–2 business days) of payment receipt (excluding weekends and statutory holidays).
* Once your package has been handed over to the postal logistics carrier, an automated confirmation email containing your official tracking number will be delivered to your inbox.

---

## 2. Estimated Delivery Timeframes

All packages are dispatched via priority tracked air services with final delivery completed by your domestic national postal authority:

| Destination Country | Carrier Partner (Last-Mile) | Estimated Delivery Time | Shipping Cost |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **United States** | **USPS Tracked Priority** | **6 – 10 Business Days** | **FREE Tracked Delivery** |
| **United Kingdom** | **Royal Mail Tracked 48** | **5 – 8 Business Days** | **FREE Tracked Delivery** |
| **Canada** | **Canada Post Expedited** | **7 – 12 Business Days** | **FREE Tracked Delivery** |
| **Australia** | **Australia Post eParcel** | **6 – 10 Business Days** | **FREE Tracked Delivery** |

*Note: Delivery timeframes are estimates and commence from the date of dispatch.*

---

## 3. Real-Time Tracking & Notifications
Every shipment includes full door-to-door tracking. You will receive:
1. An **Order Confirmation Email** immediately after placing your order.
2. A **Shipping Confirmation Email** containing your tracking number and carrier link within 48 hours.
3. An **Out for Delivery & Delivery Notification** when the package arrives at your mailbox or doorstep.

You can also track your package anytime directly on our website using your order number and email.

---

## 4. Customs, Duties & Taxes
For all orders destined for the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, and Australia:
* **USA:** Goods under $800 are exempt from import duty under Section 321 de minimis regulations.
* **UK / CA / AU:** Orders are sent via DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) compliant logistics lines whenever applicable. Customers will not encounter unexpected customs fees.

---

## 5. Incorrect Address & Undeliverable Packages
Please ensure your shipping address is accurate during checkout. If you notice an error in your address, email us at **support@folliroot.com** within 12 hours of placing the order so we can adjust it before carrier dispatch.

---

## 6. Contact Us Regarding Shipping
If your order has not arrived within 15 business days, please contact our support team at **support@folliroot.com** with your order number. We will immediately launch an inquiry with the carrier or issue a replacement unit free of charge.
