At FolliRoot Labs, customer satisfaction is our highest priority. We stand firmly behind the quality, durability, and effectiveness of our 3-in-1 Red Light Scalp Massager & Applicator Comb.

1. 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We offer a hassle-free 30-Day Return Window. You have 30 calendar days from the date your package is marked "Delivered" by the postal carrier to request a return or refund.

To be eligible for a standard return:

  • The item must be in its original packaging with all charging cables, droppers, and instruction cards included.
  • You must provide proof of purchase (order confirmation email or order number).

    • 2. Damaged or Defective Items (Instant Free Replacement)

    If your unit arrives damaged during transit or exhibits any electrical/charging defects:

  • Contact us within 7 days of delivery at support@folliroot.com.
  • Attach a short (5–10 second) video or clear photograph demonstrating the defect.
  • Our quality assurance team will inspect the proof within 24 hours.
  • Upon verification, we will immediately dispatch a brand-new replacement unit with expedited tracking at zero additional cost to you, or issue a 100% full refund to your original payment method. You will not be required to ship the defective unit back.

    • 3. How to Initiate a Standard Return

  • Send an email to support@folliroot.com with the subject line: `Return Request - Order #[Your Order Number]`.
  • State the reason for your return.
  • Our customer support desk will provide you with our designated regional returns center address and instructions.
  • *Return Shipping Costs:* For voluntary returns (e.g., changed mind), the customer is responsible for standard return postage back to our logistics inspection center. We recommend using a tracked service.

    • 4. Refund Processing

    Once your return is received and inspected at our logistics depot:

  • We will notify you via email of the approval or rejection of your refund.
  • Upon approval, your refund will be processed immediately. A credit will automatically be applied to your original payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, or Debit Card) within 3 to 5 business days, depending on your card issuer's clearing policies.
